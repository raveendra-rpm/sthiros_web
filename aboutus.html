The starting point

2002

S P R & Co. opens its doors as a chartered accountancy practice. No tech layer, no AI ambitions. Just the discipline of getting the numbers right.

SPR takes shape

2005

A five-person team sets out to do risk consulting differently: governance, assurance, and compliance work built on relationships, not volume.

Category-1 status

2012

RBI recognition as a Category-1 firm. The team grows to fifty. What started as a boutique practice starts looking like an institution.

The tech turn

2015

SPR moves into ERP consulting and starts building the technology muscle that would eventually reshape the whole business. Team crosses 150, with the first footholds outside India.

One brand, one stack

2024

The technology arm unifies under the SPR Consultech name. A dedicated Data Lab opens to push AI and analytics work. Team passes 375, spread across India, Africa, and North America, including a new base in Gaborone, Botswana, opened that September.
