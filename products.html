TRADE OPERATIONS

Keliros

An export workflow that files itself.

Keliros turns a shipment into one continuous workflow, from sales invoice to proof of delivery, replacing the patchwork of email, spreadsheets, and WhatsApp groups most export teams still run on.

Piloted inside a live steel export corridor running India to the US, with a VC-backed manufacturer as the first client.

20+ document types,
one workflow
16 wks pilot to
production
$15-50K demurrage avoided
per shipment
ADVISE

We map the shipment before we touch a screen.

  • Every handoff between sales, ops, and finance
  • The field that, if wrong, triggers a customs hold
  • Who needs visibility, and at what stage
EMBED AI

Then intelligence sits where the errors start.

  • Reads the shipping documents themselves, line by line
  • Flags a mismatch before it reaches the port
  • Predicts a delay before the carrier does
BUILD PRODUCT

What ships, and what it does.

  • A single timeline, invoice to delivery
  • A live view of what's at risk this week

Niyamos

A compliance register that updates itself.

Reads a company's entity structure and builds its complete compliance register automatically, then keeps it current as obligations change.

Piloted inside a diversified group with operations across 30-plus Indian and US states, plus a manufacturing and export footprint into Mexico.

600+ obligations tracked
for one client
2 countries under
a single register
0 spreadsheets
still required
ADVISE

We start by finding the obligations the tracker misses.

  • Every entity, jurisdiction, and filing due against it
  • Who signs off, and on what schedule
EMBED AI

Then we make the register update itself.

  • Builds the obligation calendar straight from the entity profile
  • Catches what's wrong with a filing before a regulator does
BUILD PRODUCT

What runs, day to day.

  • One register, every jurisdiction, always current
  • Evidence attached the moment a record updates
  • A single view of what's done, due, and at risk
