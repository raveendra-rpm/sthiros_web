Position: Zoho Consultant
Required Work Experience: 2–3 Years
Location: Chennai
Employment Type: Full-Time
Department: Technology / Business Applications
About the Role
We are looking for a Zoho Consultant with 2–3 years of relevant experience to join our technology team. The candidate will be responsible for understanding business requirements, designing and implementing Zoho solutions, configuring applications, and supporting clients in optimizing their business processes through the Zoho platform.
Key Responsibilities
Understand business requirements and translate them into effective Zoho-based solutions.
Configure and customize Zoho applications based on client requirements.
Work on Zoho CRM, Zoho Creator, Zoho Books, Zoho Desk, Zoho People, Zoho Analytics and other relevant Zoho applications.
Design workflows, approval processes, automation, custom modules, reports and dashboards.
Develop and maintain Zoho Creator applications, Deluge scripts and custom functions.
Integrate Zoho applications with third-party platforms and APIs.
Conduct requirement gathering, process mapping, solution design and implementation.
Perform application testing, troubleshooting and issue resolution.
Prepare functional/technical documentation, user guides and solution specifications.
Conduct user training and provide post-implementation support.
Collaborate with developers, project managers and business teams to deliver projects within timelines.
Identify opportunities for process automation and recommend suitable Zoho solutions.
Stay updated with new Zoho features, products and platform capabilities.
Required Skills
2–3 years of hands-on experience in Zoho implementation / consulting.
Strong understanding of Zoho CRM and/or Zoho Creator.
Working knowledge of Deluge scripting.
Experience in workflow automation, custom functions and application configuration.
Understanding of REST APIs and third-party integrations.
Good understanding of business processes and requirement gathering.
Strong analytical and problem-solving skills.
Good communication and client-handling skills.
Ability to work independently as well as collaboratively with cross-functional teams.
Good to Have
Zoho certifications.
Experience with Zoho Analytics, Zoho Books, Zoho Desk or Zoho People.
Experience in implementing end-to-end Zoho solutions.
Exposure to SQL, JavaScript or other programming languages.
Experience working with multiple clients or consulting projects.
Education
Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, Engineering, Business Administration or a related field.
What We Offer
Opportunity to work on diverse Zoho implementation and automation projects.
Exposure to multiple business domains and client environments.
Opportunities to work on emerging technologies and business automation.
Collaborative and growth-oriented work environment.
