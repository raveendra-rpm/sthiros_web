Real stories
from our case files

Risk

A metro rail corporation was reconciling its fare revenue against bank statements every month and believed that was enough. It wasn't. A full trace across twelve payment channels found ₹2.5-4 crore in revenue that had simply stopped arriving, unnoticed, until someone checked the whole picture instead of the summary.

AI

A large EPC conglomerate had every tool a modern site should have: scheduling software, a full ERP, daily progress meetings. None of it talked to the others. Delays on the ground were only showing up as cash crises weeks after they'd already happened. We built the system that closes that gap in real time.

Cyber

A $9.7 billion credit union had never once looked at its own technology risk as a single picture, only piece by piece. Across 1,124 risks assessed, one sat quietly inside their own ledger: a gap that could have let a manipulation go undetected in their financial reporting. It's closed now, with an owner and a deadline.

Strategy

A food manufacturer had outlets and factories that ran, by every visible measure, "fine". Over two years, we found the $3 million sitting inside "fine", in waste, inconsistent process, and untapped capacity nobody had measured because nobody thought to.
